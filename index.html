Capstone Project

LifeLens

A machine learning system that turns unstructured trauma-response audio and video into structured medical data for paramedics. Transcription and NLP models extract clinically relevant speech and intervention timestamps (e.g., CPR, intubation), while computer vision models classify injuries (e.g., swelling, abrasions, bruising) and re-identify patients, all with minimal manual input.

LifeLens is still in development in partnership with our target market as we progress the project forward as a startup.
